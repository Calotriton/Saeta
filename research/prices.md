# Arrow-building components: typical European prices (EUR)

Research date: November 2026. Prices were read directly from the shops listed in `shops.json`, mainly Franks-Castle (DE), Bearpaw Products (DE), Pijlenboogkopen (NL), MoitoArchery (ES), Bogensport Austria (AT), LUK.sk (SK), Merlin Archery (UK) and The Longbow Shop (UK).

- Prices include VAT and exclude shipping.
- UK prices were converted at roughly £1 ≈ €1.15, so treat those figures as approximate.
- Shops usually price shafts, points, nocks and feathers **per piece**. The dozen or pack prices below are those unit prices multiplied out. Bulk bags and arrow-building sets often cost less per unit.
- Prices change often and sales are common (several Spanish shops showed 15–30 % off). Use these as ballpark figures, not quotes.

## Most common brands per category (as seen in these shops)

- **Wooden shafts:** Rose City (Port Orford cedar, by far the most common), Bearpaw (cedar and spruce), BSW (Bogensportwelt own-brand spruce and pine), Wild Mountain (pine supershafts), Beier (own production), Sarmat (own production, UA). Shops often also sell unbranded Northern Pine, spruce (Fichte) or ash.
- **Traditional carbon shafts:** Bearpaw Penthalon (Bandit, Slim Line, Traditional Extreme, TimberStick), Nijora, Gold Tip (Traditional, Warrior, Entrada), Black Eagle (Outlaw, Instinct), Easton (Avance, Powerflight, Axis), Carbon Express, Skylon, Cross-X, Victory, Avalon, Sphere. Spigarelli is mostly sold in Italy and is target-focused.
- **Aluminium shafts:** Easton (XX75 Jazz, Camo Hunter, Legacy, X7 Eclipse), Merlin Gold Rush (UK).
- **Points:** Bearpaw (3D, History Point, Bearpoint), Top Hat, Saunders, Easton, Cross-X, Penthalon, Gold Tip, plus historical or replica points from shops such as HistoFakt, Bogenbau Tradition and Sarmat.
- **Nocks:** Bohning (Classic, Traditional, Signature), Bearpaw Traditional Nock, Beiter, Easton, Skylon, Gas Pro. Horn nock inserts are sold at The Longbow Shop.
- **Natural feathers:** Trueflight (most widespread), Gateway, Bearpaw, Stefan Raab (DE), Nijora (Speedline), Bodnik, Buck Trail.
- **Vanes:** Bohning (Blazer), AAE (Elite Plastifletch, WAV), Flex-Fletch, Spin Wing.
- **Fletching jigs:** Bearpaw Deluxe II, Bitzenburger, Bohning Pro Class, Avalon (Tec One, Magneto, Hexagon), Cross-X Fletcher Pro, Shocq. JoJan jigs did not appear in the shops checked, so they are uncommon in EU retail.
- **Taper tools:** Bearpaw (plastic, Parallel, Deluxe ATA, Precision), Top Hat (shaft shaper collet), Decut.
- **Glue:** Bohning Fletch-Tite Platinum, Saunders NPV, Bearpaw Archery glue and fletching tape, UHU Hart and UHU Plus Endfest (epoxy), AAE Max Impact.
- **Spine testers and scales:** ACE (Spine Master, pocket scale), Coops, A.W. Archery, Ram, Shocq (scale).
- **Cresting and wraps:** Bohning cresting jig, Bearpaw crestings, Nijora wraps, DBS wraps.

## Beginner starter shopping list

The cost estimates assume you buy loose parts from one mid-priced shop such as Franks-Castle, Bearpaw, MoitoArchery or Pijlenboogkopen, and that you build **12 arrows**. Add shipping, typically €5–10 within your own country.

### (a) Wooden arrows (cedar or pine, glue-on points, feathers)

| Item | Qty | Approx. cost |
|---|---|---|
| Spined cedar (or pine/spruce) shafts, 5/16" or 11/32", matched to your bow's draw weight | 12 (+1–2 spares) | €25–40 |
| Glue-on steel field points (taper fit) | 12 | €7–12 |
| Taper-fit traditional nocks (Bearpaw / Bohning) | 15–20 | €6–9 |
| Natural feathers, 4" shield or parabolic (3 per arrow plus spares) | 40 | €20–26 |
| Taper tool: Bearpaw plastic (cuts both 11° nock taper and 5° point taper) | 1 | €5–7 |
| Fletching jig (budget magnetic or Bohning Pro Class / Bearpaw Deluxe) | 1 | €25–80 |
| Fletching glue (Fletch-Tite Platinum) and point glue (hot-melt or 2-part epoxy) | 1 each | €10–15 |
| Optional: wood sealer or varnish, crestings or wraps | – | €10–20 |
| Optional: digital grain scale | 1 | €17–25 |
| **Total** | | **about €100–130** with a budget jig and no extras; **about €160–220** with a Bearpaw Deluxe jig, sealer and scale |

Shortcut: several shops sell ready-made wooden arrow-building kits that bundle shafts, feathers, nocks, points and sometimes tools. Examples are the Franks-Castle *Pfeilbausets*, Bearpaw's wood starter set (12 cedar shafts, 36 feathers, crestings, nocks and points, also offered with a taper tool) and the Beier *Pfeilbausets*. A kit saves time choosing parts, but the jig is usually not included.

### Tips for the website copy

1. The fletching jig is the single biggest cost, and you only buy it once. Clubs often lend one.
2. Always buy wooden shafts by spine (lb range) to match your bow's draw weight at your draw length. If the shop offers to sort shafts by spine and weight, it is worth the extra euro or two per shaft.
3. Buy 2–3 spare shafts and extra feathers. Beginners break and refletch arrows often.
4. Within the EU there are no customs charges. Buying from the UK or Ukraine (Sarmat) can add import VAT and handling fees.
